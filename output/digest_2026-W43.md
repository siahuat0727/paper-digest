### Nature Medicine（1 -> 1）

#### 1. Real-world, digital-first delivery of GLP-1 receptor agonist therapy in women.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-10-16
- 作者：John Deanfield, Jamie Lai Chun Wong, Michail Katsoulis, Emma Deanfield, Raj Thakkar, Riyaz Patel, et al.
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42888249/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42888249/)
- 命中关键词：metabolic, obesity, GLP-1, tirzepatide
- 摘要原文：Glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1RAs) have demonstrated substantial weight reduction and cardiometabolic benefit in randomized trials; however, prospective implementation evidence from digital care models and female sex community populations remains limited. Here we report outcomes from an observational study of 8,127 women initiating GLP-1RA therapy (99.8% tirzepatide) through a structured digital program incorporating mandatory biochemical monitoring, weekly video-supported follow-up and clinician-guided dose titration in the UK. Mean weight reduction was 16.1 kg (95% confidence interval 15.9 to 16.2; P < 0.001), representing 15.5% of baseline weight; 83.1% achieved ≥10% and 22.4% achieved ≥20% weight loss, with consistent responses across age, baseline weight and socioeconomic strata. Among participants with paired biomarker data, mean glycated hemoglobin decreased by 3.3 mmol mol-1 (95% confidence interval 3.1 to 3.4; P < 0.001), normalizing in 84.5% of those with baseline diabetes-range values and 96.3% with prediabetes, and lipid parameters improved significantly (reductions in total cholesterol of 0.77 mmol l-1, low-density lipoprotein cholesterol of 0.61 mmol l-1 and triglycerides of 0.47 mmol l-1). Retention was 91.4% at 6 months, with no serious treatment-related adverse events. These findings support the feasibility of delivering structured, digitally enabled obesity pharmacotherapy at scale and provide prospective real-world implementation evidence for this care model. Interpretation is limited by the observational design, absence of a comparator group, self-pay population and 6-month follow-up.

### Nature Metabolism（1 -> 1）

#### 1. NNMT enhances hyperandrogenism in polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome through epigenetic remodelling.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-10-16
- 作者：Weiwei Huang, Chuanjin Yu, Yishu Wang, Ningxia Sun, Liang Wang, Jun Ren, et al.
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42888303/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42888303/)
- 命中关键词：metabolic
- 摘要原文：Polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome (PMOS) features intertwined reproductive, endocrine and metabolic defects, yet its initiating mechanisms remain incompletely understood. Here we show that an epigenetic mechanism driven by nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT) in granulosa cells contributes to disease progression in PMOS. We observe that Nnmt overexpression in mouse granulosa cells reduces histone H3 lysine 27 trimethylation (H3K27me3), leading to increased retinoic acid receptor beta (RARB) expression. RARB, in turn, amplifies ovarian androgen synthesis. The resulting androgen excess further suppresses H3K27me3, locking cells into a feedforward loop that sustains hyperandrogenism and disrupts follicle development. Pharmacological inhibition of NNMT restores H3K27me3, disrupts the RARB-androgen feedback circuit, and ameliorates both metabolic and hormonal phenotypes in PMOS models. Our findings suggest NNMT as a regulator of PMOS through metabolic and epigenetic reprogramming. The NNMT-H3K27me3-RARB-androgen axis emerges as a mechanistic nexus linking methionine and epigenetic dysregulation to PMOS pathogenesis and represents a tractable therapeutic target.

#### 1. Prediabetes subtype-based stratification reveals distinct long-term risks for type 2 diabetes.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-10-17
- 作者：Hun Jee Choe, Wonsuk Choi, Howard H Chang, Dimple Kondal, K M Venkat Narayan, Joon Ho Moon, et al.
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42892651/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42892651/)
- 命中关键词：type 2 diabetes
- 摘要原文：Prediabetes is heterogeneous, with subtypes defined by impaired fasting glucose (IFG), impaired glucose tolerance (IGT) or HbA1c, each with different risks of progression to type 2 diabetes. We compared progression across these subtypes, and evaluated the benefit of adding insulin secretion and sensitivity to risk stratification. We analysed 7186 Korean adults aged 40-69 years from the community-based Korean Genome and Epidemiology Study, who were followed for up to 20 years with biennial (every 2 years) oral glucose tolerance tests and HbA1c. Prediabetes was classified as IFG, IGT or HbA1c-defined, and further stratified by insulinogenic index (IGI) and insulin sensitivity index (ISI). Cox regression was used to estimate diabetes risk, and multistate Markov models were used to assess annual transition probabilities and time spent in each state. Compared with normoglycaemia, the adjusted hazard ratios (aHRs) were 5.84 (95% CI 5.03, 6.79) for IFG, 4.60 (95% CI 4.17, 5.07) for IGT, and 4.68 (95% CI 4.22, 5.19) for HbA1c-defined prediabetes. The risk increased stepwise with the number of criteria met: the aHRs were 3.10 (95% CI 2.81, 3.41) for one criterion, 7.33 (95% CI 6.50, 8.28) for two criteria, and 15.3 (95% CI 12.2, 19.3) for all three. Markov models showed the shortest prediabetes duration (1.7 years) and lowest regression probability (5.4%) in those meeting all three criteria. Low IGI or ISI predicted higher diabetes risk, and dual impairment conferred greatest risk (aHR 4.06; 95% CI 3.37, 4.90). Even normoglycaemic individuals with low IGI or ISI had an elevated risk of diabetes compared to those with high IGI or ISI. Diabetes progression differs based on both glycaemic subtype and physiological subtype. Integrating IGI and ISI with conventional definitions enhances risk stratification and may help to identify high-risk individuals for earlier targeted intervention.

#### 1. Hierarchical visual prompt disentanglement for continual rare disease diagnosis.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-10-17
- 作者：Yang Hu, Yuanyuan Chen, Qingjie Zeng, Yong Xia
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42893626/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42893626/)
- 命中关键词：large language model
- 摘要原文：The automatic diagnosis of rare diseases via few-shot class-incremental learning (FSCIL) is critically hindered by severe data scarcity and the prevailing treatment of disease labels as a flat, unstructured space. This oversimplification neglects the rich taxonomic structures inherent in medical ontologies, leading to poor generalization on fine-grained subtypes. To bridge this gap, we propose Hierarchical Visual Prompt Disentanglement (HVPD), a hierarchy-guided framework for continual rare disease diagnosis. Unlike methods that use textual cues for flat semantic alignment, HVPD uses superclass and subclass category names to elicit visual-attribute semantics from a medical large language model (LLM) and converts the LLM's pre-decoding representations into hierarchical semantic anchors that guide image-conditioned visual prompt generation. We design a hierarchy-aware disentanglement mechanism that synthesizes instance-adaptive visual prompts while reducing the overlap between superclass-common patterns and subclass-specific diagnostic cues through an orthogonality-based cross-level constraint. This design alleviates the stability-plasticity dilemma by injecting structured medical priors into the visual feature space. Extensive experiments on the SD-198 (dermatology) and RFMiD (ophthalmology) datasets demonstrate that HVPD consistently improves over existing FSCIL approaches, delivering stronger fine-grained discrimination and knowledge retention under extreme few-shot regimes.
