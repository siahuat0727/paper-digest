#### 1. Sodium-Glucose Cotransporter 2 Inhibitor Use and Levels of Hemoglobin, Markers of Iron Metabolism, and Erythropoiesis: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomized Clinical Trials.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-10-24
- 作者：Yaqin Yang, Changyuan Yang, Yanmei Wang, Pien Rawee, Aleix Cases, David E Leaf, et al.
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42925557/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42925557/)
- 命中关键词：metabolism
- 摘要原文：In randomized clinical trials, sodium-glucose cotransporter 2 (SGLT2) inhibitor use was associated with increased hemoglobin and hematocrit compared with placebo. However, the magnitude and importance of this association remain unclear. To evaluate the associations between SGLT2 inhibitor use and levels of hemoglobin, hematocrit, and markers of iron metabolism and erythropoiesis compared with placebo or active comparator. PubMed, Web of Science, and the Cochrane Central Register of Controlled Trials (CENTRAL) were searched from inception to July 22, 2026. Randomized, double-masked clinical trials comparing SGLT2 inhibitor use with placebo or active comparators in patients with 1 or more of the following conditions: cardiovascular disease, diabetes, heart failure, and chronic kidney disease. Three reviewers independently extracted data and results were pooled using random-effects meta-analysis. Primary outcomes were changes in hemoglobin and hematocrit. Secondary outcomes were changes in red blood cell concentration, erythropoietin (EPO), and iron measures (serum iron, ferritin, transferrin saturation, and hepcidin). A total of 85 randomized clinical trials were analyzed (median [IQR] duration, 24 [12-32] weeks), including 70 543 participants (mean [SD] age, 62.4 [9.4] years; 25 334 female [35.9%]). Mean (SD) baseline hemoglobin was 13.6 (1.5) g/dL; baseline hematocrit was 41.5% (4.9). Seventy-eight clinical trials (67 208 participants) compared SGLT2 inhibitors with placebo, and 7 clinical trials (3335 participants) used active comparators, most commonly sitagliptin (2 trials [1158 participants]). From baseline to follow-up, hemoglobin changed by 0.55 g/dL (95% CI, 0.49 to 0.61 g/dL; P < .001) in the SGLT2 inhibitor group, and -0.08 g/dL (95% CI, -0.13 to -0.03 g/dL; P = .003) in the control group (difference, 0.60 g/dL; 95% CI, 0.55 to 0.66 g/dL; P < .001). From baseline to follow-up, hematocrit changed by 2.17% (95% CI, 1.95% to 2.39%; P < .001) in the SGLT2 inhibitor group and -0.13% (95% CI, -0.28% to 0.01%; P = .07) in the control group (difference, 2.36%; 95% CI, 2.19% to 2.52%; P < .001). Of the 6 secondary outcomes, 4 showed significant associations with SGLT2 inhibitors, including increased EPO (2.35 mIU/mL in SGLT2 inhibitor group vs 0.04 mIU/mL in control group; difference, 2.11 mIU/mL [95% CI, 0.5-3.71 mIU/mL]; P = .01) and decreased transferrin saturation (-2.2% in SGLT2 inhibitor group vs -0.10% in control group; difference, -0.84% [95% CI, -1.21% to -0.47%]; P < .001). In a meta-analysis of randomized clinical trials, compared with control, SGLT2 inhibitor use was associated with small statistically significant increases in hemoglobin and hematocrit levels. The clinical significance of these findings is unclear.

#### 1. Molecular insights into GPR55 modulation reveal an atypical activation mechanism and glycosylation-dependent efficacy regulation.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-10-24
- 作者：Tobias Claff, Chamalee Demalgiriya-Gamage, Andhika B Mahardhika, Nikolaos Angelos Stamos, Frederik Bangnowski, Xue Sun, et al.
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42926240/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42926240/)
- 命中关键词：metabolism
- 摘要原文：GPR55 is a lipid-activated G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR) implicated in metabolism, inflammation, and neurodegeneration. Lysophosphatidylinositol (LPI) species are proposed as endogenous agonists, but progress in understanding GPR55 function has still been limited by suboptimal chemical probes. Here, we present cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM) structures of GPR55 in both inactive and active states. The inactive-state structure bound to the inverse agonist CID16020046 facilitated the rational design of an improved in vitro tool compound, BI-8310. Surprisingly, mutation of a glycosylation site in extracellular loop 2 converted CID16020046 into a potent agonist, enabling co-structure determination of the active, G13-bound receptor. Comparative analysis of both states bound to the same ligand provides insights into an atypical activation mechanism that diverges from the typical helix VI outward movement observed in other GPCRs. Functional mutagenesis, N-glycoproteomics, and hydrogen-deuterium exchange mass spectrometry (HDX-MS) substantiate the findings. These results establish a basis for targeted modulation of GPR55 in pathophysiological conditions.

#### 1. Different Patterns of Progression in Nonproliferative Diabetic Retinopathy: Findings From a 10-Year Cohort Study.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-10-23
- 作者：Débora Reste-Ferreira, Inês Pereira Marques, Torcato Santos, Ana Rita Santos, Ana Cláudia Rocha, Luís Mendes, et al.
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42925124/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42925124/)
- 命中关键词：type 2 diabetes
- 摘要原文：Characterization of the 10-year progression of diabetic retinopathy in type 2 diabetes was sought. We evaluated whether noninvasive optical coherence tomography angiography (OCTA) can identify eyes that progress and may develop vision loss. Of 222 participants, 62% completed the study, with 115 reaching the 10-year visit: 27% of eyes worsened, 50% remained stable, and 21% improved, based on Early Treatment Diabetic Retinopathy Study severity grading (2% unclassified); microvascular nonperfusion, measured using noninvasive OCTA, distinguished the eyes that worsened or remained stable from those eyes that improved. Progression of microvascular nonperfusion identified by OCTA enables identification of patients at higher risk for disease progression.

### Diabetologia（1 -> 1）

#### 1. Prediabetes and diabetes as drivers of fibrosis in MASLD: roles of obesity, adipose tissue dysfunction and insulin resistance.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-10-24
- 作者：Eddison Godinez Leiva, Diana Barb, Srilaxmi Kalavalapalli, Andrea Ortiz Rocha, Nathaly Cuervo-Pardo, Anu Sharma, et al.
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42926238/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42926238/)
- 命中关键词：metabolic, obesity, insulin resistance, type 2 diabetes
- 摘要原文：Prediabetes and type 2 diabetes have been associated with more severe metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD), but not all individuals have the same risk and the metabolic drivers remain incompletely understood. To understand why, we examined the roles of obesity, adipose tissue dysfunction and insulin resistance in the development of significant liver fibrosis (fibrosis stage ≥F2) in adults across the glycaemic spectrum. We performed a prospective, cross-sectional study in a real-world setting of adults attending primary care or endocrinology/diabetes clinics. Transient elastography was used to diagnose steatosis (controlled attenuation parameter ≥274 dB/m) and clinically significant liver fibrosis (vibration-controlled transient elastography liver stiffness measurement ≥8.2 kPa). When ≥F2 was suspected, MR-based imaging or liver biopsy was used to improve stratification. MASLD and significant fibrosis were compared across glycaemic and BMI categories and severity of insulin resistance (HOMA-IR; predominantly hepatic) and adipose tissue insulin resistance (assessed by adipose tissue insulin resistance [Adipo-IR]). Among the 1446 participants, the prevalence of MASLD increased across glycaemic categories from 39% in participants with normoglycaemia, to 55% in those with prediabetes and to 68% in type 2 diabetes. Clinically significant fibrosis also increased across groups, being 1%, 6% and 12%, respectively. In prediabetes, clinically significant fibrosis was rare in lean or overweight individuals even though most had associated cardiometabolic risk factors, but this increased fourfold in individuals with obesity, particularly when associated with severe adipose tissue and systemic insulin resistance. Having obesity and type 2 diabetes had the highest prevalence of clinically significant fibrosis (16%), and again, mostly if associated with insulin resistance. Most participants with clinically significant fibrosis were in the highest quintiles of Adipo-IR or HOMA-IR (Q4-Q5). In a real-world cohort from primary care/endocrinology clinics, the prevalence of MASLD increased in a stepwise manner with glycaemic and BMI categories, but the prevalence of fibrosis did not increase in the same stepwise manner and remained rare in the absence of obesity, or in obesity without significant insulin resistance. Prediabetes was a risk factor for significant fibrosis mainly in individuals with obesity and significant insulin resistance. In the context of current guidelines, MASLD risk-stratification may be improved in primary care by establishing the metabolic impact of obesity/diabetes (i.e. assessing insulin resistance by HOMA-IR).
