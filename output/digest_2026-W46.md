#### 1. Effects of semaglutide on cardiac structure and function in heart failure with preserved ejection fraction and obesity: a pre-specified cardiac magnetic resonance substudy of the CAMEO-SEMA trial.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-11-08
- 作者：Fahime Ghanbari, Yogesh N V Reddy, Milton Packer, Arnold Ng, Rickey Carter, Michael D Jensen, et al.
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43000112/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43000112/)
- 命中关键词：obesity, semaglutide
- 摘要原文：Glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists improve symptoms in patients with obesity and heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF). We recently demonstrated that semaglutide reduces exertional pulmonary artery wedge pressure (PAWP) in obesity-related HFpEF, but potential effects of semaglutide on cardiac structure and function that may underlie these hemodynamic improvements remain unclear. In this prespecified cardiovascular magnetic resonance (CMR) substudy of the randomized CAMEO-SEMA trial, 49 participants (33 semaglutide, 16 placebo, 28 female) underwent CMR at baseline and 52 weeks, within 1 day of invasive exercise hemodynamic assessment. The prespecified primary CMR endpoint was change in left atrial (LA) reservoir strain; other prespecified CMR endpoints included LV filling dynamics, which were assessed by early filling rate indexed to end-diastolic volume (eFR/EDV). Compared with placebo, semaglutide improved LA reservoir strain (+5.7% [95% CI, +0.3 to +9.7]; P=0.037) and eFR/EDV (+0.64 s-1 [95% CI, +0.42 to +1.00]; P<0.0001). Improvements in LA reservoir strain and LV filling dynamics were associated with reductions in exercise PAWP (r=-0.46, P<0.001 and r=-0.65, P<0.0001, respectively). With respect to other prespecified CMR endpoints, semaglutide reduced LV mass, restored more physiologic LV geometry, and improved right ventricular function and biventricular myocardial mechanics. The results of this CMR substudy indicate that in patients with obesity-related HFpEF, semaglutide improves LA function and LV diastolic filling dynamics, identifying improvements in myocardial function that may contribute to semaglutide's improvement of hemodynamics. ClinicalTrials.gov number: NCT05371496.

#### 2. A gait foundation model for prediction of multisystem health phenotypes from 3D skeletal motion.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-11-06
- 作者：Adam Gabet, Sarah Kohn, Guy Lutsker, Shira Gelman, Anastasia Godneva, Gil Sasson, et al.
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42995231/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42995231/)
- 命中关键词：metabolic, foundation model
- 摘要原文：Gait is increasingly recognized as a vital sign, yet current approaches treat it as a symptom of specific pathologies rather than a systemic biomarker. Here we developed a gait foundation model for three-dimensional skeletal motion, which was recorded via a single-depth camera during 5 motor tasks, from 3,328 deeply phenotyped adults. Our purpose was to capture the full pattern of a person's movement and use it to predict health phenotypes such as grip strength and sleep quality across 18 body systems. Gait is conventionally described by engineered features (for example, calculated measurements such as cadence and stride length), which may fail to capture the fine detail of how a person moves. Our model instead translates movement into learned embeddings: sets of numbers that describe how all body joints move together over time. Learned embeddings outperformed engineered features for prediction of age (Pearson's r = 0.69), body mass index (r = 0.91) and visceral adipose tissue area (r = 0.83), and embeddings significantly predicted 1,887 of 3,210 tested phenotypes. Relative to a baseline model using age, body mass index, visceral adipose tissue and height alone, gait embeddings improved prediction in all 18 systems in males and 17 of 18 in females, as well as prediction of clinical diagnoses and medication use. Anatomical ablation analyses revealed that leg dynamics dominated metabolic and frailty predictions, and torso dynamics encoded sleep and lifestyle phenotypes. These findings establish gait as an independent, multisystem biosignal, motivating integration of gait analysis into routine clinical assessment as a scalable, passive vital sign.

#### 1. GUSB-enriched sympathetic neurons promote thermogenic recruitment through extracellular matrix remodelling in male mice.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-11-06
- 作者：Xinran Wang, Xuejuan Zhu, Junkun Jiang, Wenjing Yu, Wenlong Wu, Xuyang Jia, et al.
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42995251/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42995251/)
- 命中关键词：obesity
- 摘要原文：Sympathetic neurons stimulate adipose thermogenic recruitment, which is further sustained by extracellular matrix (ECM) remodelling; however, the direct link between sympathetic neurons and ECM remodelling is still missing. Here we show the presence of a subpopulation of β-glucuronidase (GUSB)-enriched sympathetic neurons that drive ECM reorganization essential for thermogenic programming in male mice. We demonstrate that cold exposure triggers these neurons to secrete GUSB, an ECM degradation enzyme that processes glycosaminoglycans into bioactive chondroitin sulphate (CS) fragments within the adipose tissue microenvironment. The generated CS acts through CD97 receptors on adipocytes to potentiate thermogenic activation. Importantly, we identify human GUSB variants leading to the defect of this mechanism, linking this sympathetic-ECM crosstalk to obesity. Furthermore, CS supplementation ameliorates diet-induced obesity by enhancing thermogenic recruitment in murine models. Thus, our findings uncover an ECM-remodelling group of sympathetic neurons important for thermogenic regulation and could serve as a potential strategy against obesity.

- 命中关键词：metabolism
- 摘要原文：Dairy production is constrained by low nitrogen utilization efficiency (NUE; milk N/intake N), leading to nitrogen losses and environmental pollution. Although the rumen microbiome is central to nitrogen turnover, the role of small peptides, a critical nitrogen pool, remains poorly understood. Here, we constructed a genome-resolved atlas of predicted small-peptide metabolism and integrated high-performance chemical isotope-labeling metabolomics with metagenomics in 30 dairy cows. Among 349 detected small peptides, proline-containing dipeptides were associated with higher NUE, together with an S15 peptidyl-peptidase with Xaa-Pro dipeptide-generating activity. Bacterial incubation and heterologous expression confirmed that S15.001 hydrolyzed Xaa-Pro substrates. These findings identify a microbial proline-dipeptide axis associated with NUE and provide a basis for precision nutrition strategies targeting ruminal peptide metabolism.

#### 2. Phospholipase D1 activates NAD kinase fuelling neutrophil extracellular trap formation.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-11-06
- 作者：Rahaf Albarghash, Sergio G San-Miguel, Arturo Zychlinsky, Dhiren Ferise Patel
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42995177/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42995177/)
- 命中关键词：metabolic
- 摘要原文：Neutrophil extracellular trap formation is a central innate immune defence mechanism that depends on NADPH oxidase-driven reactive oxygen species production. How NADP⁺ availability is regulated during neutrophil activation remains unclear. Using integrated lipidomic, biochemical and functional analyses in human neutrophils, we identify NAD kinase, the sole enzyme responsible for NADP⁺ synthesis, as a direct lipid-regulated effector downstream of phospholipase D1. Neutrophil activation induces rapid, phospholipase D1-dependent production of phosphatidic acid and phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate, which bind and activate NAD kinase. The resulting cytosolic NADP⁺ is reduced to NADPH by the oxidative pentose phosphate pathway and consumed by NADPH oxidase during neutrophil extracellular trap formation. Pharmacological or genetic disruption of phospholipase D1 suppresses NAD kinase activity, depletes NADP⁺ and impairs neutrophil extracellular trap formation independently of direct NADPH oxidase inhibition. These findings define a lipid-metabolic axis coupling phospholipase D1-driven membrane remodelling to NADP⁺ availability and innate immune function.

#### 1. Pregestational maternal diabetes primes neonatal beta cell dysfunction with structural remodelling, impaired calcium buffering and altered mTORC2-Gαq-PKCα signalling in a pig model.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-11-06
- 作者：Libera Valla, Minas F Schwager, Christian M Cohrs, Jan B Stoeckl, Christos Karampelias, Bachuki Shashikadze, et al.
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42995267/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42995267/)
- 命中关键词：metabolism, metabolic, type 2 diabetes
- 摘要原文：Offspring of mothers with diabetes are at increased risk of developing type 2 diabetes, and this predisposition is established during fetal life. The aim of this study was to define the functional, morphological and molecular alterations in neonatal pancreatic islets associated with maternal hyperglycaemia, with a particular focus on beta cells, an area that remains poorly understood. Given its similarity to humans in endocrine pancreas development, the pig was used as a translational animal model. Using genetically diabetic and beta cell reporter pig models, we performed functional assessments of glucose-stimulated insulin secretion in vivo and in situ using fresh pancreatic tissue slices from neonatal piglets born to hyperglycaemic mothers (PHGs) or normoglycaemic mothers (PNGs). Morphological and molecular profiling, including proteomics and single-cell RNA-seq, were performed to elucidate molecular changes reflecting the developmental impact of maternal diabetes on offspring metabolic health, with a particular focus on neonatal beta cells. PHGs were born hyperglycaemic, hyperinsulinaemic and insulin resistant, exhibiting markedly delayed glucose and insulin response curves following oral glucose administration, with more pronounced effects in male piglets. Elevated basal insulin secretion and an almost blunted glucose responsiveness of pancreatic tissue slices were associated with increased insulin and somatostatin content, expansion of beta (+35%) and delta (+47%) cell populations, and organisation into larger islet clusters. Neonatal PHG islets exhibited a molecular profile consistent with intrinsic beta cell dysfunction that partially overlapped with type 2 diabetes (e.g. RBP4 upregulation), together with alterations in glucose sensing (near absence of GLUT2), nutrient metabolism resembling congenital hyperinsulinism (HADH downregulation), and cytosolic Ca2⁺ homeostasis (near absence of the Ca2⁺ buffer calbindin D28K). In addition to an ATF4-dominated stress signature, a shift towards a distinct PHG beta cell subcluster characterised by low expression of ribosomal genes was observed, with an adaptive early-response-gene-mediated transcriptional reactivity. In contrast to the Gαs-adenylyl cyclase-cAMP-PKA signalling axis that dominated in PNG beta cells, neonatal PHG beta cells displayed distinct alterations in Gαq-PLC-PKCα signalling components, the mTORC2 target PKCα, and CREB1, suggesting a restructured signalling architecture. Our findings reveal altered islet modelling with a unique beta cell dysfunction profile that partially overlaps with, but differs from, that of type 2 diabetes. A shift towards a beta cell subcluster exhibiting an adaptive early-response-gene-mediated transcriptional reactivity and an ATF4-associated transcriptional stress signature provides insight into molecular alterations associated with fetal exposure to maternal diabetes and may contribute to the increased risk of metabolic disease in offspring.

#### 1. Muscle Lipid Localization Relates to Insulin Sensitivity: Responses to Exercise and Weight Loss Interventions.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-11-07
- 作者：Bryan C Bergman, Karin Zemski Berry, Amanda Garfield, Simona Zarini, Purevsuren Jambal, Sophia Bowen, et al.
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42995270/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42995270/)
- 命中关键词：metabolic, obesity, insulin resistance, type 2 diabetes
- 摘要原文：Subcellular lipid localization in skeletal muscle associates with insulin sensitivity in humans, yet these lipid distributions do not substantially revert after 12 weeks of insulin-sensitizing lifestyle interventions. This suggests interventions may need to occur earlier, before prediabetes or type 2 diabetes develops, or that lipid localization responds rapidly to acute changes in energy balance and physical activity that may not be captured following typical weight loss or exercise training interventions. To address this gap, we assessed the effect of acute and chronic insulin-sensitizing interventions across a spectrum of metabolic health in three separate studies. Individuals with obesity and normal glucose tolerance (NGT, n= 35, 39.4±1.0 yrs, BMI 35.7±0.7) or prediabetes/type 2 diabetes (pre/T2D, n=34, 43.8±1.3 yrs, BMI 35.3±0.7) completed 12-week interventions (combined weight loss plus exercise, weight loss alone, exercise alone, or delayed control). Endurance-trained athletes (n=20, 27.3±1.2 yrs, BMI 22.1±0.4) performed 1.5 hours of acute exercise at 50% or 70% VO2max under fed and/or fasted conditions. Muscle biopsies obtained pre/post intervention or acute exercise were fractionated into sarcolemmal, mitochondrial/ER, cytosolic, and nuclear compartments and analyzed by targeted lipidomics. Triacylglycerols were inversely associated with insulin sensitivity across all compartments. Ceramides showed inverse associations in sarcolemmal, mitochondrial/ER, and nuclear fractions, but a positive association in the cytosol. In contrast, 1,2-DAGs were positively associated with insulin sensitivity in mitochondrial/ER, cytosolic, and nuclear compartments. Neither lifestyle interventions nor acute exercise substantially altered subcellular lipid distribution. Overall, subcellular lipid localization was strongly associated with insulin sensitivity yet changed inconsistently following acute and chronic interventions, regardless of metabolic status. These data suggest that more dramatic changes in weight and fitness, and/or interventions longer than 12 weeks may be required to modify muscle subcellular lipids linked to insulin resistance.

#### 1. Leveraging AI-ECG Technology for Early Notification and Tracking of AF Development (LATENT): A Randomized Controlled Trial.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-11-07
- 作者：Chiao-Chin Lee, Wen-Yu Lin, Wei-Ting Liu, Chiao-Hsiang Chang, Yu-Lan Liu, Da-Wei Chang, et al.
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42998297/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42998297/)
- 命中关键词：artificial intelligence
- 摘要原文：Electrocardiographic analysis aided by artificial intelligence (AI) may identify atrial electrical vulnerability during sinus rhythm. The objective of this study was to determine whether AI-enabled ECG (AI-ECG) is effective for detecting atrial fibrillation (AF), atrial flutter (AFl), or prespecified pre-AF atrial findings in a randomized trial. In this single-center, open-label trial, 14 665 adults 40 to 85 years of age without previous AF/AFl were randomized to an AI-ECG-guided strategy or usual care. Participants randomized to AI-ECG who were then identified by AI as having a high-risk sinus-rhythm ECG in the preceding year were offered Holter monitoring for ≤7 days. Each monitored high-risk intervention participant was paired with an age- and sex-matched control selected before control ECGs underwent AI analysis. Matched controls received identical monitoring to reduce ascertainment bias. Reporting cardiologists were blinded to AI results and randomized assignment. The primary outcome was a composite of newly detected AF, AFl, or pre-AF atrial findings within 90 days. Pre-AF atrial findings comprised frequent premature atrial complexes, short runs of premature atrial complexes, or nonsustained AF/AFl. Among 7337 intervention and 7328 control participants, 84 (1.1%) and 78 (1.1%), respectively, were classified as high risk. Of the 84 high-risk intervention participants, 48 (57.1%) underwent monitoring, as did 48 matched controls. The primary outcome occurred in 54 intervention participants (0.74%) versus 35 control participants (0.48%; odds ratio, 1.54 [95% CI, 1.01-2.37]; P=0.046). Hypothetical reclassification of 1 intervention event to a nonevent changed the primary analysis P value to 0.057. The intervention effect on detection of the primary composite outcome differed by baseline AI-ECG risk (Pinteraction<0.001): 32.1% versus 2.6% among high-risk participants and 0.4% versus 0.5% among low-risk participants. AF/AFl detection (0.3% versus 0.2%) and antiarrhythmic therapy initiation at 180 days (2.3% versus 2.4%) did not differ significantly between the intervention and control groups. Participant-reported monitoring-related adverse events, including skin reactions and discomfort, occurred in 22.9% of monitored intervention participants versus 18.8% of matched controls. AI-ECG-guided monitoring resulted in a modest increase in the detection of new AF, AFl, or pre-AF atrial findings. However, these findings were fragile statistically and sensitive to reclassification of 1 event. LATENT (Leveraging AI-ECG Technology for Early Notification and Tracking of AF Development) demonstrates the clinical application of AI-ECG risk stratification to guide targeted rhythm surveillance, providing a foundation for larger trials evaluating its impact on patient outcomes. URL: https://www.clinicaltrials.gov; Unique identifier: NCT06847932.
