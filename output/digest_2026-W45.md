#### 1. Insulin Sensitivity and Insulin Secretion Differentially Affect Birthweight and Postpartum Glucose.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-10-31
- 作者：Diana C Soria-Contreras, Emily A Rosenberg, Hannah E Shames, Kaitlyn James, Deepti Pant, Lydia Shook, et al.
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42965914/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42965914/)
- 命中关键词：insulin resistance
- 摘要原文：We tested whether early pregnancy insulin resistance and insulin deficiency are associated with infant birthweight percentile and postpartum glucose in women with diabetes risk factors. This was a secondary analysis of data from the prospective Study of Pregnancy Regulation of Insulin and Glucose (SPRING). Participants were enrolled at 7-14 weeks' gestation and underwent a 2-hour oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT) with hourly glucose and insulin measures. Exposures were first-trimester (7-14 weeks' gestation) log-transformed insulin resistance and insulin secretory response, determined using the Matsuda index and Stumvoll first-phase estimate. Primary outcomes were birthweight percentile and 2-hour OGTT glucose at 6-24 weeks postpartum. Secondary outcomes included fasting, 30-min, and 1-hour OGTT glucose postpartum. We used covariate-adjusted linear regression models to quantify associations between standardized exposures and outcomes. Among 150 participants, greater first-trimester insulin resistance was associated with higher birthweight percentile (adjusted β: 5.11%, 95% CI: 0.04, 10.18 per 1 SD decrease in log Matsuda index), but not with postpartum glucose. Lower first-trimester insulin secretory response was not associated with birthweight percentile, but it was associated with higher postpartum 2-hour OGTT glucose (adjusted β: 13.96 mg/dL, 95% CI: 7.24, 20.69 per 1 SD decrease in log Stumvoll estimate), as well as glucose at the fasting, 30-min, and 1-hour post-OGTT time points postpartum. Insulin resistance and insulin deficiency in the first trimester are differentially associated with maternal and fetal outcomes. Assessing the underlying mechanisms of hyperglycemia in pregnancy may enable more personalized risk assessment.

#### 2. Body Mass Index-Specific Central Adiposity and Risk of Incident Type 2 Diabetes Among Aging Women.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-10-31
- 作者：May A Beydoun, Hind A Beydoun, JoAnn E Manson, Lesley Tinker, Jack Tsai, Thomas E Rohan, et al.
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42963906/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42963906/)
- 命中关键词：metabolic, type 2 diabetes
- 摘要原文：Body mass index (BMI) does not capture central adiposity, which may provide additional information for type 2 diabetes (T2D) risk stratification. To evaluate BMI-specific waist circumference (WC) categories in relation to incident T2D and assess their performance across independent cohorts. Prospective analyses included 133,620 Women's Health Initiative (WHI) participants free of T2D at baseline, randomly assigned to training (70%; n=93,463) and test (30%; n=40,157) samples. External validation included the Health and Retirement Study (HRS; n=2,885) and Healthy Aging in Neighborhoods of Diversity across the Life Span (HANDLS; n=1,037). Cox models estimated T2D risk across BMI and BMI-specific WC categories. Discrimination was assessed using Harrell's C-statistic. In WHI, T2D risk increased progressively across BMI categories (adjusted hazard ratios [HRs], 1.51-3.30) and joint BMI-WC categories (HRs, 1.40-4.43). Within BMI strata, higher WC generally identified greater T2D risk. Associations were directionally consistent in HRS and HANDLS. Age modified associations, with stronger gradients at younger ages, whereas race and ethnicity interactions were not statistically significant. Model discrimination was modest (C-statistics approximately 0.60-0.75). BMI-specific WC categories refine adiposity-related T2D risk stratification beyond BMI alone but provide modest individual-level discrimination. WC may complement BMI in identifying women warranting more comprehensive metabolic risk assessment rather than serving as a stand-alone T2D prediction tool.

#### 1. Predicting persistent fever in patients with cancer receiving antibiotics: a machine learning approach.

- 来源：PubMed
- 日期：2026-10-31
- 作者：Gernot Pucher, Sanjat Kanjilal, Kevin Kopp, Aman Deep, Till Rostalski, Martin Schuler, et al.
- 链接：[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42966761/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42966761/)
- 命中关键词：machine learning, electronic health record
- 摘要原文：Persistent fever after initiating intravenous antibiotics is common in hospitalized patients with cancer and often leads to additional imaging and antimicrobial escalation. Early identification of patients unlikely to remain febrile could provide additional information during clinical reassessment. We developed and externally validated machine learning models to predict persistent fever 48-72 h after antibiotic initiation using electronic health records from two academic hospitals. Adult cancer patients with fever within 24-48 h of treatment were included. Models incorporated demographics, comorbidities, laboratory results, vital signs, and temperature patterns. The best-performing model achieved an AUROC of 0.82 (95% CI 0.79-0.84) internally and 0.78 (95% CI 0.77-0.79) externally. Temperature-related variables were the dominant predictors. Decision curve analysis demonstrated positive net benefit across much of the evaluated threshold range. Retrospective assessment suggested that approximately one quarter of antibiotic escalations and chest CT scans occurred in patients correctly predicted not to remain febrile, highlighting the potential contextual value of fever predictions. These findings indicate that short-term fever persistence can be predicted with clinically meaningful accuracy using routine data. However, clinical use should be limited to an adjunctive role rather than standalone rule-out decision-making, and prospective studies are required to evaluate safety, clinician uptake, and real-world impact.
